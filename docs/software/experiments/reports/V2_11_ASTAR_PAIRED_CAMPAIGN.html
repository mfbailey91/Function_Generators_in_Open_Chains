1. Does the four-bar change A* expansion vs the gearbox?

Primary metric: mechanism-mean of log((Nfb+1)/(Ngb+1)), then hierarchical bootstrap across pairs. The effect is small and stably negative in production. Confirmation (15 stratified pairs at 96×96) is consistent in sign but its CI includes zero.

2. Pair-level distributions

Expansion contrast is modest and sometimes reverses at the task level (only 43.9% of tasks have Nfb < Ngb). Actuator travel is unambiguous: four-bar LU is shorter on every paired task because the gearbox is span-matched on a shared Q lattice while U travel differs.

Each point is one (pair, task). LQ is identical by construction (mean 0.765 on both sides). Mean expansions: four-bar 1174, gearbox 1201. Mean LU: four-bar 2.49, gearbox 3.06.

Limitations

  • Expansion-count contrast is small. Path-quality / actuator-travel contrast is large and one-sided.
  • Tasks are nested inside pairs. Do not treat 1288 task log-ratios as iid.
  • Production IDs are the V2.10 completed set (161), not a newly powered A* sequential stop.
  • Grid anisotropy remains (four-connected shared Q lattice).
