V4.2D optimality-reference and gearbox-control report

Under the frozen V4.2C center-goal representation, span-matched gearbox and four-bar mechanisms possess different exact actuator-travel references, and planner families approach those mechanism-specific references at different finite-budget rates. This report is descriptive and is not a global ranking and not a mechanism ranking.

1. What is the exact reference?

The V4.2C worker represented the Cartesian disk by planar-2R IK lifts of the disk center only. Candidate keys are disk_center::elbow_up and disk_center::elbow_down. Primary reference rows: 100. Stage C rows joined: 6200. Already-satisfied: 1240.

2. What did the mechanism change?

Negative ΔJC* means the four-bar has the lower center-goal actuator optimum. J*_B is a representation-sensitivity control only.

3. How quickly did optimizers approach it?

RRT* and BIT* use wall-time checkpoints 0.05, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 1.00 s. Already-satisfied queries are excluded from these plots.

4. Did the mechanism change discoverability?

Paired contrasts are index-matched, not CRN. Equal seeds are not a common-random-number estimator.

5. Why was the final solution suboptimal?

Goal-selection regret uses the reconstructed direct cost of the final selected center-IK family. Checkpoint-level goal identity is not retained in V4.2C and is not inferred here.

6. Fixed-budget and projection controls

FMT is an independent sample-count run and is not placed on the RRT*/BIT* wall-time axis. KPIECE-U/Q/X is a projection diagnostic; a lower final cost is not faster convergence. PRM retains the sequential-goal workaround label. RRTConnect is a first-feasible control.
